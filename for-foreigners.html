What a shaken bill is actually made of

車検代金は4つで構成されます

Guides written for foreign residents usually quote one big number for shaken and leave it there. It is really four separate things, and only the first two are ours. Here is the whole picture before you ask us for anything.

下の料金表に載っているのは、このうち上の2つ（固定料金）です。

Inspection fee車検基本料Our labour — the 59-point statutory inspection法定点検59項目・点検整備記録簿の作成 Fixed固定料金
Agency fee代行料We take the car to the transport bureau for you陸運支局への持込代行 Fixed固定料金
Statutory costs法定費用Paid to the government, not to us — insurance, weight tax, inspection stamp自賠責保険料・重量税・検査印紙代（非課税） Variesお車ごとに変動
Repairs needed必要な整備Only what the car needs to pass — quoted and approved before we start保安基準適合に必要な部品交換と作業 Variesお車ごとに変動

Statutory costs are set by your car's weight and age, so no shop can quote them lower than another. Repairs are quoted in writing and we start only once you say yes. Nothing appears on your bill that you have not seen first.

法定費用はお車の重量と年式で決まるため、どの工場でも同額です。必要な整備は事前にお見積りをご提示し、ご承認後に着手します。

Yes. Our staff handle customer communication in English. You can message us in English on LINE, call us, or use the contact form, and you will get a reply in English. Yes. The easiest way is LINE. Send us a message with (1) your car's make and model, (2) what you need, such as shaken, repair, oil change, or tyres, and (3) a photo of your shaken sticker or the car registration (車検証 / shakensho) if you have it. We will reply in English with a quote and available dates. You never need to write or speak Japanese at any step.

You are welcome to drop by during our hours. Please understand that we may not be able to look at your car immediately, because other customers' bookings are already scheduled. If you can, message us on LINE before you come so we can tell you when someone will be free. For urgent problems such as warning lights, strange noises, or a flat tyre, come straight in and we will do our best to help.

Bring three things: your car, your 車検証 (shakensho, the vehicle registration certificate, usually kept in the glove box), and your current 自賠責保険 certificate (jibaiseki, the compulsory insurance, usually in the same folder). If you are not sure which documents you have, bring the whole folder and we will sort it out with you. We are a government-certified workshop (認証工場), so the inspection and repairs can all be handled here. We will explain every line of the quote in English before any work starts.

Yes. We regularly service Y-plate vehicles and have helped many SOFA-status owners through shaken. We handle the inspection and any repairs needed to pass. Base-side registration and paperwork remain your responsibility with your base vehicle registration office, but we can tell you in English exactly what the car needs mechanically and what the inspection covers. Message us on LINE with your plate type and vehicle details to get started.

Bring the inspection result sheet with you, or send a photo on LINE. It lists exactly which items failed. We will go through each item in English, explain what it means, and give you a quote to fix only what is needed to pass. Once the repairs are done, we handle the re-inspection. A failed shaken is common and fixable. The important thing is to act before the current shaken expires.

It depends on who is in the car. For one or two people, a kei car (軽自動車, the small yellow-plate cars) is the practical choice: lower tax, lower insurance, and it fits the parking spaces in Naka-ku, Motomachi, Yamate, and Honmoku. For families, a compact car with sliding doors is what most of our customers end up choosing. Sliding doors matter more than you expect in tight Japanese parking lots when you are getting children in and out. If you want something with character for weekend trips, we are an authorized Suzuki sub-dealer and can talk you through the Jimny, new or used. Tell us your family size, your parking situation, and your budget on LINE, and we will suggest two or three realistic options.

Often, yes. Two years of car ownership in Japan can cost less than two years of car rental or car-sharing, especially for families. When you leave, we can help you sell the car. We buy used cars ourselves and can give you an honest appraisal and explain the deregistration process in English. Tell us your planned departure date when you buy, and we will keep it in mind when recommending a car.

Yes. Hillside Auto is an authorized Suzuki sub-dealer. We can order any current Suzuki model, including the Jimny, Jimny Sierra, Spacia, Solio, and Swift, and handle the whole process in English: grade and option selection, the order, registration, insurance, and delivery. After delivery, servicing and warranty work are done here, so you deal with the same people from purchase onward.

Shaken cost has two parts. The first is the legal fees: vehicle weight tax, compulsory insurance, and the inspection stamp fee. These are set by the government and are the same at any shop. The second is the inspection and maintenance work, which depends on the car's age, condition, and what needs to be repaired to pass. Because of this, we do not publish a single all-in price. Our workshop fee for each vehicle class is on our price list, and the legal fees and any repairs come on top. Send us your car's details and a photo of your shaken sticker on LINE and we will give you an itemized quote in English, with the legal fees and the workshop fees shown separately, before you commit to anything.

Yes. We accept credit cards and cashless payments (QR code and e-money) for repairs, shaken work, and vehicle purchases. One exception: the legal portion of shaken fees, meaning the vehicle weight tax and the compulsory insurance, must be paid in cash. We will show this part separately on your quote so you know the amount in advance.
