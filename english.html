Shaken, in English

Vehicle inspection
Q

How much will it cost?

Our fee (inspection fee + taking the car in for the test) starts at ¥35,200 for a kei car and ¥41,800 for a standard car, tax included. With statutory costs, the total starts at about ¥62,960 (kei), ¥79,260 (standard) and ¥80,460 (imported). Repairs are extra.

Q

Will you add work without asking?

No. If anything needs replacing, we give you a quote and explain it first. We only start once you agree.

Q

What do I need to bring?

Your car, your 車検証 (shakensho) and your 自賠責 (jibaiseki) insurance certificate. Not sure which is which? Bring the whole folder and we will sort it out with you.

Q

How can I pay?

Credit card, QR payments and e-money are all accepted. The statutory costs (weight tax and compulsory insurance) must be paid in cash.

Q

How long does it take?

Allow at least two days. We can lend you a courtesy car for ¥1,100 a day, which covers insurance. We have only a few, so please ask when you book.

Q

When should I book?

You can take shaken up to two months before your current one expires without shortening the next one. August–September and February–March are our busiest months, so book early if your date falls then.

Repairs and servicing

Warning light, strange noise, flat tyre, oil change — tell us what is happening on LINE. A photo helps. We quote first and only start once you agree.

Walk-ins are welcome, but we may not be able to look at your car straight away. For urgent problems such as warning lights, strange noises or a flat tyre, come straight in and we will do our best to help.

Buying a car

We are an authorised Suzuki sub-dealer for new cars, and we sell used cars too. If what you want is not in stock, we can search nationwide auctions for a model, year and budget that fit.

The paperwork — registration, the parking space certificate (車庫証明, shako shomeisho) and name transfer — is handled for you.

Selling your car when you leave Japan

We buy cars of any make, age or condition, including cars that do not run. Valuation is free and we quote on the spot.

We handle the name transfer or de-registration, so you do not have to. Tell us your leaving date when you contact us.

What we can and can't do

So you don't waste a trip

We can

  • Shaken, servicing and repairs for Japanese and imported cars
  • Bodywork, paint and scratch repair
  • Compulsory insurance (自賠責, jibaiseki) renewal
  • Registration, name transfer and other paperwork
  • New and used car sales, and buying your car

We can't

  • Tesla vehicles — we do not have the dedicated equipment
  • Towing, or collecting a car that has broken down. We also cannot call a tow company for you
  • Mobile work — we cannot come to your home or to a port yard
  • US base personnel: insurance other than 自賠責 (for example PDI), and procedures that have to be done on base. Everything else is fine
