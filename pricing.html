Labour rates for inspection, servicing and registration — every figure includes tax.

表示価格はすべて税込・技術料（工賃）です。作業内容によっては、別途部品代がかかります。金額は最低料金で、お車の状態・車種により変わる場合があります。作業前に必ずお見積りをご提示します。

All prices include consumption tax and cover labour only — parts are billed separately. Each figure is a starting price and may vary with the vehicle and its condition. A written estimate is always provided before work begins.
